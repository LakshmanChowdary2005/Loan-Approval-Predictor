About CreditPulse AI System Architecture

A full-stack, enterprise-grade Machine Learning Underwriting and Financial Risk Analysis platform engineered for the ShadowFox intermediate internship task.

🎯 Project Scope & Engineering Deliverables

  • Supervised Machine Learning: End-to-end training and evaluation of 5 classification models (Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Gradient Boosting, Extra Trees).
  • Anti-Leakage Preprocessing: Stratified 80/20 train/test splits, median & mode imputation, StandardScaler, and OneHotEncoder encapsulated in reproducible Scikit-Learn pipelines.
  • Explainable AI (SHAP): Transparent tree feature attributions delivering both global significance rankings and local per-applicant waterfall decision explanations.
  • Debt Affordability Stress Testing: Mathematical amortization, Debt-To-Income (DTI) computation, and safe borrowing capacity guidelines.
  • Auditable Data Persistence: Transactional SQLite audit logging storing every prediction ID, timestamp, and feature vector.
  • Institutional Reports: Automated PDF underwriting memos via ReportLab and formatted Excel workbooks via OpenPyXL.
