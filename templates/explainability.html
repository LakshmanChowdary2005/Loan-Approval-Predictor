🛡️ Algorithmic Accountability & Methodology

Shapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP)

SHAP connects cooperative game theory with optimal credit decision attributions. By evaluating all possible feature coalitions, it assigns each feature an exact marginal contribution toward shifting the applicant's likelihood of approval above or below the baseline dataset average.

Key Explanatory Principles

  • Credit History Dominance: Accounts for ~45% of total predictive weight. Prime credit history (1.0) is the single most vital positive driver.
  • Household Solvency Cushion: Combined applicant & co-applicant earnings mitigate high loan commitments.
  • Asset Location Demographics: Semiurban collateral demonstrates high historical clearance rates.
Statutory Fair Lending Notice: In compliance with the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) and Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) guidance, automated underwriters must provide adverse action notices citing exact statistical factors rather than opaque black-box outcomes.
